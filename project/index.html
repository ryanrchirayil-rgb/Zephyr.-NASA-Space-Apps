BEYOND THE MISSION

Not everything returns home.

Every space mission leaves something behind. Some machines complete their mission and remain on distant worlds. Others become silent witnesses to humanity's greatest achievements.

DESTINATION 01

THE MOON

Humanity's first steps beyond Earth. Discover the machines and instruments left behind on our closest celestial neighbour.

Apollo Lunar Module

The descent stages of the Lunar Modules (like Apollo 11's Eagle) were left behind as launch pads.

Lunar Roving Vehicles(LRV)

Three electric "moon buggies" driven by astronauts. Parked permanently on the Moon.

ALSEP Scientific Packages

Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Packages (seismometers, retroreflectors, etc.) left to do science after astronauts departed.

Surveyor Landers

Robotic probes sent in the 1960s to prove soft landings were possible.

Apollo 14:Abandoned Equipment

Apollo 14 left behind its Lunar Module descent stage, scientific instruments, ALSEP experiments and equipment cart, preserving a lasting record of humanity's exploration of the Moon.

Apollo17

Apollo 17 left behind its lunar descent stage, Lunar Roving Vehicle, scientific instruments and other equipment at the Taurus-Littrow site, marking the final human exploration of the Moon.

LCROSS

LCROSS deliberately crashed its Centaur rocket stage and spacecraft into the Moon, helping NASA confirm the presence of water ice near the lunar south pole.

DESTINATION 02

MARS

A world of robotic exploration. Meet the machines that helped us understand the Red Planet.

Sojourner Rover

The first rover on Mars (1997), delivered by the Pathfinder lander. Now permanently parked.

Spirit & Opportunity

Twin rovers that operated for years. Both are now inactive monuments to Mars exploration.

Viking 1 & 2 Landers

The first U.S. landers to successfully operate on Mars (1976), remaining exactly where they touched down.

Phoenix & InSight Landers

Phoenix dug for water ice; InSight listened for marsquakes. Both eventually lost power.

Perseverance Rover

The parachute, heat shield, and protective aeroshell were discarded during Perseverance’s landing on Mars.

Curiosity Rover

Curiosity discarded its cruise stage, heat shield, parachute, backshell and sky crane during its landing on Mars.

Ingenuity Helicopter

The first aircraft on another planet. Sustained rotor damage and became a stationary artifact.

A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

Milestones of Exploration

1966

Surveyor 1 successfully lands on the Moon.

1969

Apollo 11 lands humans on the Moon.Apollo 12 deploys the first ALSEP scientific experiment package.

1971

Apollo 15 carries the first lunar rover.Explored the Fra Mauro region and left behind its lunar module descent stage, ALSEP experiments and equipment.

1972

Last crewed Moon landing; left behind the lunar module descent stage, lunar rover and scientific equipment.

1976

Viking 1 and 2 become the first successful U.S. landers to operate on Mars.

1997

Sojourner explores the surface of Mars.

2004

Spirit and Opportunity land on Mars and begin exploring its surface.

2008

Phoenix lands on Mars and discovers water ice beneath the surface.

2009

Deliberately crashed into the Moon, helping confirm water ice near the lunar south pole.

2012

Curiosity lands in Gale Crater to investigate Mars's ancient environment.

2018

InSight lands on Mars to study its internal structure and detect marsquakes.

2021

Ingenuity completes its first powered flight on Mars.Perseverance lands in Jezero Crater.

2024

Ingenuity's mission ends after rotor damage.
